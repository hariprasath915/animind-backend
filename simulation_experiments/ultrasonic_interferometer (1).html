Method. The quartz transducer at the base of the cell drives the liquid column at a fixed RF frequency. When the reflector-to-crystal gap d satisfies d = n·(λ/2), reflected and incident waves reinforce and the piezoelectric loading peaks — read on the microammeter as a maximum anode current. Successive maxima are spaced by λ/2, so wavelength λ = 2 × (average spacing between maxima), and the ultrasonic velocity in the medium v = λ × f. This is a physics simulation for educational purposes; real bench results will vary with cell alignment, temperature and signal noise.
