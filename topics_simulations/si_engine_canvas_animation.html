📖 Difficult Words, Explained
slider-crank mechanism
A machine part that turns spinning motion into back-and-forth sliding motion.
crank
A rotating arm or lever attached to a spinning shaft.
connecting rod
A metal piece linking the rotating crank to the sliding piston.
line of stroke
The straight path that the sliding part moves along.
piston
A solid cylinder that slides back and forth inside a tube.
displacement
The distance an object has moved from its starting point.
